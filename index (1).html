About

Why I do this work

I started this journey because something kept bothering me. There are countless investment options out there, but too often they are handed out without anyone asking whether they suit the person buying. That is a problem worth solving, and it is the reason I am here.

Friends and family kept showing me plans they didn't understand and hadn't asked for. Nobody seemed to be asking the simple questions first: why this plan, and why for you? I decided to build a practice where the customer's needs come first.

My approach is simple: I always profile before I recommend. Before we talk about a single fund, I want to understand:

  • What you are saving for, and by when
  • How much risk you can genuinely stay calm with
  • Your income, expenses and existing commitments
  • Your family responsibilities and future plans

Only then do I suggest anything, and I explain my reasons in plain language, so you always know what you own and why it is there. No jargon, no pressure, and no promises of quick riches.

Investing isn't about chasing trends. It is about steady habits, realistic expectations, and a plan that fits your life. I will also stay with you after you invest, reviewing progress, answering questions, and helping you stay patient when markets get noisy.

Services

How I can help

1

Financial Profiling

Every plan starts with a conversation about you, not a fund. We talk through what you are saving for and by when, how much risk you can genuinely stay calm with, your income, expenses and commitments, and your family's needs. This profile becomes the foundation for everything that follows, so any suggestion is built around your life and not around a product.

2

Goal-Based Planning

Saving works better when each rupee has a purpose. Whether it is a child's education, a home, a career break or retirement, we give each goal a timeline and a realistic monthly amount. I explain which type of mutual fund may suit each goal and why, so you can see how the pieces fit together and judge the plan for yourself.

3

SIP Planning and Setup

A Systematic Investment Plan, or SIP, lets you invest a fixed amount every month, so saving becomes a habit rather than a decision you keep postponing. I help you choose an amount that fits your budget, set it up properly, and adjust it as your income or goals change. Market values move up and down, and returns are not guaranteed.

4

One-Time Investing

If you have a bonus, a maturing deposit or savings sitting idle, we think through how to invest it thoughtfully. We look at your goals, your time horizon and how comfortable you would be if the value dipped, then decide whether to invest all at once or in stages. The aim is a decision you can stay calm with.

5

Portfolio Review

Already invested but unsure what you own? I review your existing mutual fund holdings in plain language, showing what each one does, how they overlap, and whether they still match your goals. You then decide what to keep, change or stop, with my reasoning laid out clearly. Changes happen only when there is a clear reason.

6

Ongoing Support and Reviews

Investing does not end on the day you start. I stay in touch to review your progress, answer questions, and help with paperwork such as nominations and account updates. When markets get noisy, I help you separate real reasons to act from fear and headlines, so you can stay patient and keep to the plan you made.

Resources

Learn at your own pace

You should never have to take anyone's word for it, including mine. The Association of Mutual Funds in India, known as AMFI, runs an official website at amfiindia.com where you can learn how mutual funds work, read investor awareness material, and look up information published for the public. I encourage every client to explore it before, during and after investing.

AMFI also runs the "Mutual Funds Sahi Hai" investor awareness campaign, which explains basic ideas such as what a mutual fund is, why risk and time matter, and how regular investing works. Its website also lists the daily value (NAV) of mutual fund schemes, and lets you locate a registered distributor. You can use it to check that anyone offering you mutual fund services holds a valid registration number, called an ARN. Mine is ARN-370426.

Use these resources to ask better questions, and bring those questions to me. I will answer in plain language, and I will tell you honestly when something is not right for you.
